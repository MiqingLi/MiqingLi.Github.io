Pareto-based SBSE frequently returns a set of non-dominated solutions. Evaluating those sets requires more than counting how many solutions were found; the evaluator must consider convergence, spread, diversity, and practical relevance.

## Results Summary

- Evaluation should use indicators that match the intended comparison and the decision-making context.
- Visual inspection and indicator-based comparison should support each other, especially on complex or high-dimensional trade-off surfaces.
