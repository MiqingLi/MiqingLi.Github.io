Automated product disassembly supports remanufacturing and recycling by planning how end-of-life products can be decomposed efficiently. The optimisation task can involve throughput, cost, precedence constraints, and resource allocation.

## Selected Work Themes

- Robotic disassembly line balancing.
- Multi-objective modelling of efficiency and resource constraints.
- Search algorithms for practical industrial disassembly scenarios.
