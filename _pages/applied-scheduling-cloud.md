Cloud workflow scheduling typically requires balancing execution time, monetary cost, service quality, and resource constraints. Multi-objective evolutionary search can help explore these trade-offs.

## Selected Work Themes

- Scheduling workflows across heterogeneous cloud resources.
- Balancing makespan and cost under practical constraints.
- Comparing population-based search with other scheduling strategies.
