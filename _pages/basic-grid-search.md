Grid-based evolutionary search exploits grid-based fitness and selection criteria to strengthen selection pressure toward promising regions while maintaining broad and uniform distribution among solutions.

## Results Summary

- Grid-based methods were designed to better handle many-objective problems where standard selection pressure is weak.
- Experiments on 8-objective and 10-objective DTLZ-style problems show how grid-based search can maintain solution distributions in high-dimensional objective spaces.
