Direct observation of solution sets becomes infeasible when an optimisation problem has more than three objectives. Visualisation is therefore important both for algorithm designers comparing search behaviour and for decision makers choosing preferred solutions.

## Topic Pages

- [Visualised Test Problems]({{ '/basic-research/visualisation/visualised-test-problems/' | relative_url }})

## Selected Work Themes

- Constructing test problems whose decision-space geometry mirrors high-dimensional objective-space geometry.
- Improving parallel coordinates for reading and comparing many-objective solution sets.
- Reducing objective dimensionality to support clearer visualisation of final solution sets.
