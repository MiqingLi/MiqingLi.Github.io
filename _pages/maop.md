MaOP collects resources related to many-objective optimisation and multi-objective optimisation research.

## Resources

- Benchmark and test-problem materials for many-objective optimisation.
- Quality indicators and evaluation resources.
- Algorithm materials connected to the research topics on this site.
- Visualisation resources for examining high-dimensional solution sets.
