The multi-line distance minimisation problem suite is designed so that Pareto optimal solutions in a two-dimensional decision space have a similar geometric distribution to their images in a higher-dimensional objective space.

This makes it possible to visually inspect how a solution set is distributed in high-dimensional objective space by looking at its corresponding low-dimensional decision-space pattern.

## Results Summary

- The suite supports visual comparison of many EMO algorithms on 4-objective and 10-objective instances.
- It helps reveal when an algorithm appears strong numerically but misses important structural parts of the solution set.
