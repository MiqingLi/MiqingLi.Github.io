In evolutionary multi-objective optimisation, environmental selection can be viewed as an archiving process: new solutions are compared with existing ones, and the population or archive is updated.

## Topic Pages

- [Archiving Failed on Simplex Shapes]({{ '/basic-research/multi-objective-archiving/archiving-failed-on-simplex-shapes/' | relative_url }})

## Selected Work Themes

- Constructing simple sequences where established archiving methods fail.
- Investigating optimality and monotonicity of archiving methods.
- Studying deterioration in archive quality, including cases where discarded solutions dominate many solutions retained by popular archivers.
