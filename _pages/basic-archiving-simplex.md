This work constructs seven sequences of solutions with extremely simple Pareto-front shapes but carefully chosen input orders. The sequences expose failure modes in five archiving methods used by established EMO algorithms: NSGA-II, IBEA, SMS-EMOA, MOEA/D, and NSGA-III.

## Results Summary

- Even very simple 1D and 2D simplex Pareto-front shapes can cause established archiving rules to behave poorly.
- The results challenge the assumption that popular Pareto-, indicator-, and decomposition-based archivers are reliable on simple input structures.
