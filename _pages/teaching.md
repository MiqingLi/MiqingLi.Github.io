## Student Projects

My research interests lie in evolutionary computation, multi-objective optimisation, and their applications. Students may choose a project from the areas below or propose a project related to these interests.

### Randomised Algorithms for Combinatorial Optimisation

Possible problem domains include:

- Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP)
- Knapsack Problem (KP)
- Job Shop Scheduling Problem (JSSP)
- Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP)
- Timetabling
- Bin packing
- Allocation
- NK-landscape
- Quadratic Assignment Problem

### Multi-Objective Combinatorial Optimisation

Possible projects include:

- Designing effective algorithms for a specific multi-objective combinatorial problem.
- Investigating which search algorithms perform well on a specific multi-objective combinatorial problem.
- Studying how evolutionary algorithms scale on one class of combinatorial problems.
- Analysing search operators such as crossover and mutation, including how they scale.

### Visualisation and Decision Making

When a problem has more than three objectives, observing optimisation results directly becomes difficult. Related projects include:

- Reducing dimensionality according to correlations between objectives.
- Modifying visualisation tools, such as parallel coordinates, for clearer observation.
- Selecting representative solutions for assisted decision making.
- Comparing decision-making approaches and their relationship to multi-objective optimisation.

### Other Project Directions

- Comparing randomised search techniques with MIP solvers such as CPLEX and Gurobi across different problem scenarios.
- Using population-based search, such as genetic algorithms, to discover unexpected patterns and designs in art.
