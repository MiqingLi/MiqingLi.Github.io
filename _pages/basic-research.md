My basic research interests principally lie in multi-objective optimisation. This work has two connected directions.

First, I am interested in optimisation problems that are common in the modern world but difficult to solve: problems with many conflicting objectives, tiny feasible spaces, many local or global optima, and evaluations that are expensive to run.

Second, I study foundational issues in multi-objective optimisation, including how to evaluate the solution sets returned by optimisation algorithms, how to maintain populations and archives during search, and how to make decision-making easier through visualisation.
