This survey reviews 100 quality indicators for multi-objective solution sets. It analyses the strengths and weaknesses of representative indicators, discusses issues in quality comparison and evaluation, gives recommendations for designing and selecting indicators, and identifies future research directions.

## Practical Focus

- Which indicator properties matter in different evaluation scenarios.
- Where commonly used indicators can mislead.
- How to choose indicators in light of the problem geometry, objective dimensionality, and decision-maker preferences.
