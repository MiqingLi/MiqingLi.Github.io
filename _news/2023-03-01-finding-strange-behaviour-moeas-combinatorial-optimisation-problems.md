March 2023 - Our paper on finding a "strange" behaviour of MOEAs on combinatorial optimisation problems has been accepted by [GECCO'23](https://gecco-2023.sigevo.org/). This work reported an interesting phenomenon that different executions of an MOEA may end up in very different areas in search space. Congratulations to Xiaofeng and Xiaochen!
