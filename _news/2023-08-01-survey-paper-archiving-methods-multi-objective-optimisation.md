August 2023 - Our survey paper, collaborated with Dr [Manuel Lopez-Ibanez](https://lopez-ibanez.eu/), on archiving methods in multi-objective optimisation has been accepted by IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation. This work conducts a systematic study of archiving methods in multi-objective optimisation from a general theoretical perspective. Congratulations to Manuel and Xin!
