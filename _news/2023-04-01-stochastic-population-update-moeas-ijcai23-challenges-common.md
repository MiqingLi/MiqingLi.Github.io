April 2023 - Our paper, collaborated with Dr [Chao Qian](http://www.lamda.nju.edu.cn/qianc/)'s group, on stochastic population update for MOEAs has been accepted by [IJCAI'23](https://ijcai-23.org/). This work challenges the common practice of updating the population in a deterministic, greedy way in EMO, and analytically proves that introducing randomness in population update can help. Congratulations to Chao, Yawen and Chao!
