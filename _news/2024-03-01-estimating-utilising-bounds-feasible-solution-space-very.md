March 2024 - Our work, collaborated with Prof [Guofu Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/), on estimating and utilising the bounds of feasible solution space for a very constrained multi-objective optimisation problem, test resource allocation of software testing, has been accepted by ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology. Congratulations to Guofu!
